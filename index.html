Three quick rules

  1. 1

    Stop

    Don't rush. Scammers want you to act fast. Real banks and government agencies give you time.

  2. 2

    Check

    Use the checkers below, or call the ScamShield Helpline 1799.

  3. 3

    Never share

    Never give your OTP, password, Singpass or bank PIN to anyone, even if they say they are police or bank staff.
